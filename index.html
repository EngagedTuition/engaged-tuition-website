Who We Are

Why I started
Engaged Tuition

Hi! I'm Harry, the founder of Engaged Tuition. What started as a few sessions tutoring students gradually grew into something much bigger. Over time, more and more students (and parents) encouraged me to expand because they valued not only the academic progress being made, but also the friendly, supportive environment that each lesson provided. That balance between strong academic teaching and genuine encouragement is what Engaged Tuition is built around.

Alongside tutoring, I also manage the day-to-day running of Engaged Tuition: coordinating tutors, organising sessions, and making sure everything runs smoothly for families. Reliability, communication and organisation are incredibly important to us, because consistency is what allows students to feel supported and make long-term progress.

Every tutor at Engaged Tuition shares the same approach: strong subject knowledge, up-to-date exam experience and a real commitment to helping students grow in confidence as well as ability. We want students to feel comfortable asking questions, supported throughout the process and proud of the progress they make along the way.
