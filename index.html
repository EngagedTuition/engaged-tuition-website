Who We Are

Why I started
Engaged Tuition

Hi! I'm Harry, the founder of Engaged Tuition. What started as a few sessions tutoring students gradually grew into something much bigger. Over time, more and more students (and parents) encouraged me to expand because they valued not only the academic progress being made, but also the friendly, supportive environment that each lesson provided. That balance between strong academic teaching and genuine encouragement is what Engaged Tuition is built around.

Alongside tutoring, I also manage the day-to-day running of Engaged Tuition: coordinating tutors, organising sessions, and making sure everything runs smoothly for families. Reliability, communication and organisation are incredibly important to us, because consistency is what allows students to feel supported and make long-term progress.

Every tutor at Engaged Tuition shares the same approach: strong subject knowledge, up-to-date exam experience and a real commitment to helping students grow in confidence as well as ability. We want students to feel comfortable asking questions, supported throughout the process and proud of the progress they make along the way.

Meet the Team

Your tutors

Current university students with top grades and a genuine passion for helping others reach their potential.

Harry
Biology · Chemistry

Hello! I'm Harry, the founder of Engaged Tuition and a Biology and Chemistry tutor currently studying Biochemistry at the University of Edinburgh with a predicted First-Class degree. I have been tutoring for the past three years, teaching students from KS3 through to A-Level. Throughout this time, I have developed a teaching style centred around one key principle: true understanding. I strongly believe that when students genuinely understand what they are learning, confidence naturally follows; confidence is often the difference between simply memorising content and achieving excellent results.

My lessons are designed to be engaging, supportive, and interactive, ensuring that students remain focused and motivated throughout every session. I aim to create an environment where students feel comfortable asking questions, making mistakes and developing confidence in their own abilities. Patience, encouragement and reliability are at the heart of the way I teach.

In subjects such as Biology and Chemistry, exam technique is just as important as subject knowledge. I place a strong emphasis on helping students master exam technique, understand how marks are awarded, and develop the skills needed to approach questions with confidence.
